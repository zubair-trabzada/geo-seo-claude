Buyer FAQ

The questions buyers actually ask

How do I know the machine's real condition?
Every machine gets a documented inspection: true hours, a condition report, and a run-off video on request. We'd rather lose a sale than ship a surprise.
Do you handle rigging and freight?
Yes. We coordinate teardown, loading, and nationwide shipping so the machine arrives ready to set. We've shipped to 48 states.
Is there any warranty?
Inspected machines carry a 30-day functional guarantee on the items listed in the condition report. Extended coverage is available on most units.
How do I pick the right tonnage?
Tell our assistant your part size, material, and cavitation and we'll point you to the right tonnage and shot-size range — or talk it through on a call.
Can you help with financing?
We work with equipment-finance partners and can provide CMEA/USPAP appraisals your lender will accept.
How fast can it ship?
In-stock machines typically ship within 5–10 business days of payment, depending on rigging schedule and destination.

Tell us what you need. Our assistant answers in seconds, any time of day, and our team takes it from there.
