The Problem

Sara Uy built one of the most visible personal brands in B2B sales. But AI can't see any of it.

When ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews visit sellingsara.com, they don't find a sales training brand with 330,000 followers and Forbes recognition. They find 1.27 MB of JavaScript and zero readable text.

The Wix Thunderbolt platform assembles every page client-side after JavaScript executes — a step AI crawlers do not perform. The result: no headings, no service descriptions, no bio, no schema, no structured data of any kind.

The primary domain has zero confirmed Google-indexed pages. The sitemap returns 404. There's no llms.txt file. No meta description. No Person schema linking Sara Uy to her 85K LinkedIn, 170K TikTok, or 79K Instagram.

Create an llms.txt file immediately to bypass JavaScript rendering. Add Person + Organization schema with sameAs links. Fix the broken sitemap. Publish dedicated pages for each of the six workshops. Key Findings

What We Found

14 issues identified, ordered by severity. Each directly impacts your GEO score.

Critical

JavaScript Rendering Blocks All Content

Every page delivers 1.27 MB of JavaScript with zero readable text. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot cannot extract any content. All page content assembles client-side after JS execution — a step AI crawlers do not perform.

Critical

No llms.txt File Exists

No llms.txt at /llms.txt or /llms-full.txt. This single file would let AI models bypass JavaScript rendering entirely by reading structured plaintext. Highest-ROI fix available.

Critical

Sitemap Declared But Returns 404

robots.txt points to a sitemap that doesn't exist. Without a valid sitemap, AI crawlers and search engines cannot discover or enumerate pages to index.

Critical

Zero Structured Data on Any Page

No JSON-LD, Microdata, or RDFa detected anywhere. No Person schema connects Sara Uy to LinkedIn (85K), TikTok (170K), or Instagram (79K). Schema score: 2/100.

Critical

Primary Domain Has Zero Indexed Pages

Only the newsletter subdomain appears in Google. The main sellingsara.com has zero confirmed indexed pages — the clearest signal of total AI crawler access failure.

High

No Privacy Policy or Terms of Service

Email collection without a privacy policy violates GDPR/CCPA and fails Google Quality Rater trust standards. Legal liability for a New York business.

High

Homepage Missing Meta Description

One of the few HTML elements readable before JavaScript executes. Its absence means AI systems cannot summarize the site from raw HTML alone.

High

No Wikipedia Entity for Sara Uy

Wikipedia is the strongest entity recognition signal for AI. Notability threshold appears met: Forbes 30 Under 30, documented $500K+ revenue, 330K+ following.

High

Zero Reddit Presence

Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Claude heavily index Reddit for practitioner recommendations. Cold calling and social selling are actively discussed in relevant subreddits.

High

No Dedicated Content Pages

All six workshops, coaching, and about content return 404. No blog, no case studies, no indexable resources. All educational content lives on platforms Sara doesn't own.

Medium

No Open Graph or Twitter Card Tags

Missing OG and Twitter Card meta tags affect social sharing appearance and reduce identity signals for AI crawlers.

Medium

No YouTube Channel

YouTube is indexed directly by Google AI Overviews and cited by Perplexity. Sara's video-native content makes this a natural low-effort extension.

Medium

No FTC Disclosures for Brand Partnerships

Sponsored content from Pipedrive and Luster isn't disclosed per FTC guidelines. Both a legal compliance issue and E-E-A-T trustworthiness gap.

Low

AI Crawlers Not Explicitly Named in robots.txt

All crawlers inherit the wildcard Allow rule. Best practice: explicitly list GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, and Google-Extended to prevent accidental blocking.
